# Dual-license strategy pending institutional and coauthor approval

No reuse license is granted by this draft repository at present. All rights are reserved until definitive terms are approved.

The intended release strategy is:

1. a non-commercial academic research license permitting inspection, reproduction of the published analyses, and modification for research and teaching, subject to attribution and citation requirements; and
2. a separate commercial license requiring prior written authorization and, where appropriate, negotiated financial terms.

The definitive license text must be reviewed with the coauthors and the relevant KU Leuven intellectual-property or technology-transfer office before the repository or a Zenodo archive is made public.

Publication of the source code does not imply permission for commercial use, redistribution, sublicensing, or incorporation into commercial products.
